# Visual Design Style Guide - Modern Helpdesk (Freshdesk Inspired)

## Theme & Target Audience
- **Theme:** Professional Customer Support SaaS. Clean, organized, trustworthy, and efficient.
- **Inspiration:** Freshdesk, Zendesk, Intercom.
- **Audience:** Customer support agents, managers, and administrators.

## Color Palette
- **Primary:** #2C5CC5 (Freshdesk Blue) - Used for primary actions, links, and active states.
- **Secondary:** #12344D (Deep Navy) - Used for sidebars, headers, and strong contrast elements.
- **Accent:** #264966 (Muted Blue) - Used for secondary text and borders.
- **Success:** #107C10 (Green) - Used for success states, "Open" status.
- **Warning:** #F18D13 (Orange) - Used for "Pending" status, medium priority.
- **Danger:** #D93025 (Red) - Used for "High" priority, destructive actions.
- **Background:** #F3F5F7 (Light Gray) - Main application background to reduce eye strain.
- **Surface:** #FFFFFF (White) - Cards, tables, and content areas.

## Typography
- **Font Family:** Inter, system-ui, -apple-system, sans-serif.
- **Headings:** Bold, dark slate (#183247). H1 (24px), H2 (20px), H3 (16px).
- **Body:** Regular (14px), slate gray (#475867).
- **Labels:** Medium weight (12px), uppercase or muted.

## Layout & Components
- **Structure:** Dashboard-style layout. Sidebar navigation (if applicable) or top navigation bar.
- **Cards:** White background, subtle shadow (shadow-sm), rounded corners (rounded-lg), padding (p-6).
- **Buttons:**
  - Primary: Solid blue, rounded-md, px-4 py-2, hover:bg-blue-700 transition.
  - Secondary: Outline gray or ghost, hover:bg-gray-50.
- **Inputs:** White background, border-gray-300, focus:ring-2 focus:ring-blue-500 rounded-md.
- **Tables:** Clean rows, hover effects (hover:bg-gray-50), subtle borders.

## Visual Design Principles
- **"Wow" Factor:** Smooth transitions between pages. Micro-interactions on hover.
- **Images:** High-quality avatars for users/agents. 
- **Icons:** Use Lucide-vue-next or similar clean SVG icons (simulated via text/emoji if library missing, but preferably SVGs).
- **Whitespace:** Generous spacing to prevent clutter.
- **Z-Index Strategy:**
  - Modals: z-50
  - Dropdowns: z-40
  - Sticky Headers: z-30
  - Content: z-0

## Image Usage
- **Hero/Dashboard:** Use abstract SaaS illustrations or clean office photography.
- **Avatars:** Real human faces for agents and contacts.
- **Empty States:** Friendly illustrations.
