# Visual Style Guide - Online Courses App (Coursera Inspired)

## Theme & Target Audience
- **Theme:** Professional Online Education (MOOC)
- **Reference:** Coursera
- **Target Audience:** Professionals, Students, Lifelong Learners seeking career advancement.
- **Visual Tone:** Trustworthy, Clean, Academic yet Modern, Global.

## Color Palette
- **Primary:** `#0056D2` (Deep Blue) - Used for primary buttons, links, branding.
- **Secondary:** `#FFFFFF` (White) - Card backgrounds, content areas.
- **Accent:** `#E2E8F0` (Light Slate) - Borders, dividers.
- **Background:** `#F5F7F8` (Off-white/Light Gray) - Main page background to let content cards pop.
- **Text:**
  - Headings: `#1F2937` (Dark Gray/Almost Black)
  - Body: `#4B5563` (Medium Gray)
  - Muted: `#9CA3AF` (Light Gray)
- **Success:** `#16A34A` (Green) - For enrollment success, completion.
- **Warning/Star:** `#F59E0B` (Amber) - For ratings.

## Typography
- **Font Family:** System UI / Sans-serif (Inter, Roboto, Open Sans).
- **Sizes:**
  - H1: `text-4xl font-bold` (Hero titles)
  - H2: `text-2xl font-semibold` (Section headers)
  - H3: `text-xl font-medium` (Card titles)
  - Body: `text-base`
  - Small: `text-sm` (Metadata, breadcrumbs)

## Layout & Components
- **Container:** Centered max-width container (`max-w-7xl mx-auto px-4`).
- **Cards:**
  - White background (`bg-white`).
  - Rounded corners (`rounded-lg` or `rounded-xl`).
  - Subtle shadow (`shadow-sm`), increasing on hover (`hover:shadow-md`).
  - Image thumbnails on top, content below.
- **Buttons:**
  - **Primary:** Blue background, white text (`bg-blue-700 text-white rounded hover:bg-blue-800`).
  - **Secondary:** White background, blue border (`border border-blue-700 text-blue-700 hover:bg-blue-50`).
- **Navigation:**
  - Sticky top navbar.
  - Clean white background with shadow.
  - Logo on left, Search in middle, Auth/Menu on right.
- **Images:**
  - High-quality real photos for course thumbnails and hero backgrounds.
  - Use `object-cover` for consistent aspect ratios.

## Interactivity
- **Hover Effects:** Cards lift slightly (`transform hover:-translate-y-1 transition`).
- **Micro-interactions:** Button clicks showing active state.
- **Modals:** Center-aligned with backdrop blur for Cookie/Permission dialogs.
