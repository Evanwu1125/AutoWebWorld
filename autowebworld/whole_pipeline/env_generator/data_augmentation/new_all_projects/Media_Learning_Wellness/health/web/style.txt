# Visual Style Guide - Teladoc Health Inspired App

## Theme & Audience
- **Theme**: Telemedicine / Healthcare
- **Reference App**: Teladoc Health
- **Target Audience**: Patients seeking convenient, professional medical care.
- **Visual Tone**: Trustworthy, Clean, Professional, Calming, Modern, Accessible.

## Color Palette
- **Primary**: #005DAA (Deep Blue - Trust, Professionalism)
- **Secondary**: #009CDE (Light Blue - Friendliness, Action)
- **Accent**: #722282 (Purple - Mental Health, Premium)
- **Background**: #F8F9FA (Light Gray - Cleanliness)
- **Surface**: #FFFFFF (White - Card backgrounds)
- **Text**: #333333 (Dark Gray - Readability)
- **Error**: #D32F2F (Red)
- **Success**: #2E7D32 (Green)

## Typography
- **Font Family**: 'Inter', system-ui, -apple-system, sans-serif
- **Headings**: Bold, Clear, Dark Blue (e.g., text-2xl font-bold text-[#005DAA])
- **Body**: Regular, Readable, Dark Gray (e.g., text-base text-gray-700)
- **Buttons**: Semi-bold, rounded-full or rounded-md.

## Layout & Components
- **Container**: Max-width 1200px, centered.
- **Cards**: White background, subtle shadow (shadow-md), rounded-lg (rounded-xl).
- **Navigation**: Clean top bar with logo and clear menu items. Mobile-responsive.
- **Buttons**:
  - Primary: Background Primary Blue, Text White, Hover Darker Blue.
  - Secondary: Border Primary Blue, Text Primary Blue.
- **Inputs**: Rounded borders, clear focus states (ring-2 ring-blue-500).
- **Images**:
  - High-quality photos of doctors, patients, and lifestyle.
  - Rounded corners for images.
  - Use ImageGetter for realistic placeholders.

## Interceptors & Modals
- **Cookie Consent**: Bottom fixed banner or centered modal. Friendly, not intrusive.
- **Location Permission**: Centered modal, explanation of why location is needed (finding local providers).
