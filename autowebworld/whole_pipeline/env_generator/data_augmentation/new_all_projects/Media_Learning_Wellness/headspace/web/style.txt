# Visual Style Guide - Meditation & Mindfulness App

## Theme & Inspiration
- **Reference App:** Headspace
- **Target Audience:** General public seeking mindfulness, stress relief, and better sleep.
- **Vibe:** Calm, playful, friendly, approachable, soothing.
- **Visual Tone:** Soft, rounded, illustrative, warm.

## Color Palette
- **Primary:** #F47D31 (Warm Orange - energetic yet friendly)
- **Secondary:** #3C4E75 (Deep Blue - calming, trust)
- **Background:** #FDFBF7 (Off-white/Cream - softer than pure white)
- **Text:** #333333 (Dark Grey - readable, softer than black)
- **Accents:**
  - Pastel Yellow: #FFD56B
  - Pastel Blue: #A8D0E6
  - Pastel Pink: #F8B7CD
  - Success Green: #88C999
  - Warning Red: #FF8B8B

## Typography
- **Headings:** 'Nunito', 'Quicksand', or rounded sans-serif. Bold, friendly.
- **Body:** 'Open Sans', 'Lato', or clean sans-serif. Readable, comfortable line height (1.6).

## UI Components
- **Buttons:**
  - Pill-shaped (fully rounded corners).
  - Primary: Orange bg, white text, subtle shadow.
  - Secondary: Transparent with colored border or soft colored bg.
- **Cards:**
  - Large border-radius (rounded-2xl or rounded-3xl).
  - White background with soft, diffused shadow (shadow-lg).
  - Hover effects: Slight lift (transform -translate-y-1).
- **Inputs:**
  - Rounded corners (rounded-xl).
  - Light grey border, soft focus glow.
- **Modals:**
  - Centered, backdrop blur (glassmorphism hint).
  - Friendly illustrations if possible.

## Layout
- **Container:** Centered max-width (max-w-4xl for dashboard, max-w-md for forms).
- **Spacing:** Generous whitespace (p-6, p-8, gap-6). Breathable designs.
- **Grid:** Responsive grid for lists (1 col mobile, 2-3 col desktop).

## Imagery
- **Strategy:** Use ImageGetter for realistic but calming images.
- **Keywords:** "meditation illustration", "peaceful nature", "calm person", "sleeping cat", "focus study", "abstract soft shapes".
- **Hero:** Large, inviting imagery on HOME.
- **Thumbnails:** Consistent aspect ratio (square or 4:3), rounded corners.

## Interactive Elements
- **Hover:** Smooth transitions (duration-300).
- **Feedback:** Visual feedback on selection (border color change, scaling).
- **Z-Index:**
  - Nav: z-20
  - Modals: z-50
  - Cookie: z-[10000]
  - Permission: z-[9999]
