# Visual Design Style Guide - Tumblr Inspired

## Theme & Vibe
- **Core Theme**: "Microblogging Energy" - Deep, immersive, artistic, and fluid.
- **Reference**: Tumblr (Dark Mode default).
- **Target Audience**: Creatives, fandoms, artists, Gen Z/Millennials.
- **Visual Tone**: Expressive, eclectic, raw but polished, "cool" chaos.

## Color Palette
- **Primary Background**: #001935 (Deep Navy) or #121212 (Modern Dark). Let's go with a Modern Deep Navy/Black blend: `bg-slate-900` (#0f172a).
- **Secondary Background** (Cards/Inputs): `bg-slate-800` or White `bg-white` (classic post style).
- **Primary Accent**: #00B8FF (Electric Blue) - Buttons, Links, Active states.
- **Secondary Accent**: #FF62CE (Hot Pink) - Likes, Highlights, Special actions.
- **Text**: #FFFFFF (Primary), #9DA6AF (Secondary/Metadata).

## Typography
- **Headings**: 'Helvetica Neue', Arial, sans-serif. Bold, tight tracking.
- **Body**: Readable sans-serif.
- **Quotes**: Serif (Georgia) for quote posts to give that classic blog feel.

## Layout Patterns
- **Dashboard**: Central vertical feed, single column width (approx 540px-600px max width for posts), centered.
- **Explore**: Masonry Grid (Waterfall layout) - critical for visual heavy content.
- **Navigation**: Sticky sidebar (desktop) or Bottom bar (mobile).
- **Modals**: Glassmorphism overlays (`backdrop-blur-md`).

## Component Styling
- **Buttons**: Pill-shaped (rounded-full) or slightly rounded (rounded-md). High contrast.
- **Inputs**: Transparent background with bottom border OR filled pills.
- **Post Cards**: Sharp corners or slightly rounded (`rounded-sm`). Distinct separation between posts.
- **Images**: Full bleed where possible. High quality real images.

## Image Usage Strategy
- Extensive use of real photography via ImageGetter.
- "Hero" headers for Blog Overview.
- Avatars for every user interaction.
- Masonry grid images for Explore page.
