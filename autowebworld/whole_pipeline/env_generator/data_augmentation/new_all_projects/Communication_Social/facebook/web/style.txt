# Visual Style Guide - Facebook Clone

## Theme & Target Audience
- **Reference App:** Facebook (Web)
- **Target Audience:** General public, social networking users seeking connection and content sharing.
- **Visual Tone:** Clean, professional, user-friendly, and content-centric. The design should feel familiar, trustworthy, and modern.

## Color Scheme
- **Primary:** Facebook Blue (#1877F2) - Used for primary buttons, active states, links, and branding.
- **Secondary:** Gray (#E4E6EB) - Used for secondary buttons, backgrounds, and hover states.
- **Accent:** Green (#42B72A) - Used for success states and "new" indicators.
- **Background:** Light Gray (#F0F2F5) - Main application background color.
- **Surface:** White (#FFFFFF) - Used for cards, posts, modals, and headers.
- **Text:**
  - Primary: #050505 (Almost Black)
  - Secondary: #65676B (Dark Gray)
- **Error:** Red (#F02849)

## Typography
- **Font Family:** system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif.
- **Weights:** Regular (400), Medium (500), Bold (600/700).
- **Sizes:**
  - Headings: 24px (h1), 20px (h2).
  - Body: 15px (standard post text), 13px (metadata).

## Layout & Components
- **Card Design:** All content (posts, profiles, events) resides in white cards with `rounded-lg` and subtle shadow `shadow-sm`.
- **Navigation:** Fixed top navigation bar (white, shadow-md) with centered icons and right-aligned profile/settings.
- **Grid System:** Responsive 3-column layout on desktop:
  - Left: Navigation/Menu (fixed)
  - Center: Feed/Main Content (scrollable)
  - Right: Sponsored/Contacts (fixed)
- **Spacing:** Generous padding (p-4) within cards; gap-4 between elements.
- **Interactive Elements:**
  - Buttons: Pill-shaped or rounded-md. Primary buttons have blue bg/white text. Secondary buttons have gray bg/black text.
  - Inputs: Light gray background (#F0F2F5) with rounded-full shape for search and comments.

## Image Usage Strategy
- **Hero/Background:** High-quality, realistic images for covers and events.
- **Avatars:** Circular user profile pictures (`rounded-full`).
- **Post Content:** Large, full-width images within feed cards (`w-full object-cover`).
- **Icons:** Minimalist, filled or outlined SVG icons.
- **Implementation:** Use `ImageGetter` to fetch diverse, realistic images for users, posts, and events.
