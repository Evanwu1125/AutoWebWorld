# Visual Style Guide - JD.COM Inspiration

## Theme Overview
This application mimics the JD.COM e-commerce platform. The design focuses on a "Mall" experience: trustworthy, vibrant, and content-rich. The target audience is general consumers looking for quality products and reliable service.

## Color Scheme
- **Primary Brand Color**: JD Red (#E1251B) - Used for primary buttons, price text, and key accents.
- **Secondary Color**: Dark Gray (#333333) - Used for primary text, headings.
- **Background Color**: Light Gray (#F6F6F6) - Page background to separate content blocks.
- **Surface Color**: White (#FFFFFF) - Card backgrounds, headers, content areas.
- **Accent Color**: Orange (#FF9900) - Used for special tags, ratings.

## Typography
- **Font Family**: -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, "Helvetica Neue", Arial, sans-serif.
- **Sizes**:
  - Headings: 18px - 24px (Bold)
  - Body: 14px (Regular)
  - Small/Meta: 12px (Gray)
  - Price: 20px (Bold, Red)

## Layout & Spacing
- **Container**: Centered, max-width 1200px for desktop. Full width for mobile.
- **Grid System**: 
  - Product lists: Grid layout (2 cols mobile, 4-5 cols desktop).
  - Sections separated by 12px - 20px whitespace.
- **Cards**: White background, subtle shadow (shadow-sm), rounded corners (rounded-lg).

## Component Styling
- **Buttons**:
  - Primary: JD Red background, White text, rounded-full or slightly rounded (rounded-full).
  - Secondary: White background, Gray border, Black text.
- **Inputs**: Gray border (#CCCCCC), focus state with Red glow.
- **Navigation**: White sticky header, clear iconography.
- **Images**: High quality, object-cover, often with white padding for products.

## Visual Tone
- Professional yet energetic.
- "Busy" but organized - high information density is expected in e-commerce.
- Usage of badges (e.g., "Self-Operated", "Flash Sale") to create urgency.

## Image Usage Strategy
- **Hero Banners**: Colorful, promotional banners on Home.
- **Product Thumbnails**: Clean product shots on white backgrounds.
- **Category Icons**: Graphical icons for navigation menus.
- **Realism**: Use ImageGetter to fetch real product images (laptops, groceries, phones) to make the mall feel stocked.
