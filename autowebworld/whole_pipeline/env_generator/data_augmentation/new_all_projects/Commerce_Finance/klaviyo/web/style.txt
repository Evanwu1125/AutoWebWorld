# Visual Style Guide - Email & SMS Marketing App (Klaviyo-inspired)

## Theme & Tone
- **Theme**: Professional Marketing Automation Platform. Clean, data-driven, trustworthy, yet modern and energetic.
- **Reference**: Klaviyo.
- **Tone**: "Wow factor" professional. Crisp typography, generous whitespace, subtle shadows, and high-quality imagery.

## Color Palette
- **Primary**: #2563EB (Royal Blue) - For primary actions, active states.
- **Secondary**: #1E293B (Slate 800) - For navigation, headings.
- **Background**: #F8FAFC (Slate 50) - Light, clean background.
- **Surface**: #FFFFFF (White) - Cards, modals.
- **Accent**: #10B981 (Emerald) - Success states, positive metrics.
- **Text**: #334155 (Slate 700) - Body text.
- **Muted**: #94A3B8 (Slate 400) - Secondary text, placeholders.

## Typography
- **Font Family**: Inter, system-ui, sans-serif.
- **Headings**: Bold, tight tracking.
- **Body**: Readable, comfortable line height (1.5).

## Layout & Components
- **Glassmorphism**: Subtle use on overlays/modals (backdrop-blur-md).
- **Cards**: Rounded-xl, shadow-sm hover:shadow-md transition-all.
- **Inputs**: Rounded-lg, border-slate-200, focus:ring-2 focus:ring-blue-500.
- **Buttons**:
    - Primary: Blue bg, white text, rounded-lg, hover:bg-blue-600.
    - Secondary: White bg, border, slate text, hover:bg-slate-50.
- **Images**: extensively used for "Wow" factor.
    - Hero images on Home.
    - Thumbnails for Campaigns/Flows/Forms.
    - Avatars for user profiles.

## Visual Details
- **Micro-interactions**: Hover effects on cards (lift up), smooth transitions on buttons.
- **Z-Index Strategy**:
    - Modals: z-[10000]
    - Dropdowns: z-50
    - Sticky Headers: z-40
    - Content: z-0
