# Walmart Clone - Visual Design Style Guide

## Theme & Target Audience
- **Reference Application**: Walmart (Retail/E-commerce)
- **Target Audience**: General consumers, families, value-conscious shoppers.
- **Visual Tone**: Friendly, accessible, clean, trustworthy, energetic (spark of yellow).

## Color Scheme
- **Primary**: Walmart Blue (`#0071DC`) - Used for header, primary buttons, links, active states.
- **Secondary**: Spark Yellow (`#FFC220`) - Used for accents, "Add to Cart" highlights, special offers.
- **Background**: White (`#FFFFFF`) for main content, Light Gray (`#F3F4F6`) for section backgrounds.
- **Text**: Dark Gray (`#2E2F32`) for headings, Medium Gray (`#46474A`) for body text.
- **Error**: Red (`#DE1C24`)
- **Success**: Green (`#2A8703`)

## Typography
- **Font Family**: 'Inter', system-ui, sans-serif.
- **Headings**: Bold, tight tracking.
  - H1: 2.25rem (36px), Bold
  - H2: 1.5rem (24px), SemiBold
  - H3: 1.25rem (20px), Medium
- **Body**: 1rem (16px) base size, readable line height (1.5).

## Layout & Component Styling
- **Global**: Full-width header, centered container for main content (max-w-7xl).
- **Cards**:
  - White background, rounded-lg (0.5rem).
  - Subtle shadow (`shadow-sm`) on rest, elevated shadow (`shadow-md`) on hover.
  - Product images are focal points.
- **Buttons**:
  - Primary: Blue background, white text, rounded-full or rounded-md.
  - Secondary: White background, blue border/text.
  - Ghost: Transparent background, underlined text on hover.
- **Forms**:
  - Input fields with rounded corners, gray borders (`border-gray-300`).
  - Focus state: Blue ring (`ring-blue-500`).
- **Navigation**:
  - Top bar with Logo, Search, Account, Cart.
  - Mega-menu or dropdowns for departments.

## Image Usage Strategy
- **Hero Images**: High-quality lifestyle shots for Home (families, shopping, seasonal).
- **Product Images**: Clean product shots on white/transparent backgrounds.
- **Thumbnails**: Consistent aspect ratio (square or 4:3).
- **Icons**: Simple, outlined icons (using SVG or simulated via CSS shapes if necessary, or emoji for simplicity in rapid prototyping, but preferring SVG).

## Interactive Elements
- Hover effects on product cards (lift up).
- Smooth transitions for dropdowns and modals.
- Ripples on button clicks.
